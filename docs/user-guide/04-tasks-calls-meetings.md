# Getting things done: tasks, calls, meetings

## Tasks and follow-ups

**Who:** everyone for their own tasks; admins and cofounders also for other people.

A task is something that has to get done, optionally with a due time and a follow-up: when it's due, Alfred
checks with whoever owns it and tells whoever asked.

**Ask Alfred**

> "Add a task: renew the car insurance by the end of the month."
> "Ask Max to send the invoice by Friday noon and follow up."
> "What's on my task list?"
> "The insurance task is done."

**Follow-ups**

- At the due time Alfred asks the owner: on **Telegram** (with **Done** / **Not yet** buttons) or by **phone**.
- If it isn't done, he asks again daily, up to 3 times.
- The person who asked hears when it's done, when the owner says "not yet" or gives a new date, and once if
  there's still no answer.

**On the dashboard:** **Tasks → List** shows tasks you own or asked for, with a form to add one; **Tasks →
Calendar** shows tasks on their due date.

## Calls on your behalf

**Who:** admins and cofounders.

Alfred can phone people in the registry for you, now or later, and report back.

**Ask Alfred**

> "Call Max and ask whether he's coming on Saturday."
> "Call Erika tomorrow at 9 and remind her about the contract."
> "Which calls are planned?" / "Cancel the call to Max."

**What happens**

- Alfred introduces himself as calling on your behalf, asks or tells what you wanted, and says goodbye.
- No answer: he tries again twice, 10 minutes apart. A voicemail: he leaves a short message.
- Afterwards you get a summary on Telegram, and the call is in the Archive.
- He respects the other person's quiet hours; say "it's urgent" if it can't wait.
- "Call me at 7" works for everyone, as a reminder by phone.

**On the dashboard:** **Tasks → Schedule a call** (now or later), or **Call** next to a person on **Personnel**
(admins). Planned calls are under **Upcoming**, finished ones under **Archived** (searchable).

## Calls to businesses and numbers outside the registry

**Who:** admins and cofounders.

Alfred can ring a business or anyone outside the registry for **one concrete task**, such as booking a table or
asking about opening hours.

> "Call Restaurant Sonne on 031 000 01 00 and book a table for 4 this Friday at 19:00 under Mustermann."

**The rules**

- Alfred reads back the number, the task and what he may share, and **dials only after your yes**.
- Only concrete tasks; he declines vague or social calls ("just have a chat").
- He says he's an AI assistant calling on your behalf and that the call is recorded, shares only what you
  allowed, and never agrees to costs or commitments.
- Swiss and Liechtenstein numbers by default; no premium-rate numbers; Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 19:00 (later
  requests wait for the next window); at most 10 such calls a day per person and 3 a day per number.
- Every request becomes a task on your list; the outcome completes it and you get a report on Telegram.
- If the other side says "don't call again", the number is blocked.

## Meetings

**Who:** admins and cofounders organise; anyone in the registry can be invited.

> "Find an hour with Max and Erika next week."

1. Alfred looks at your calendar and the calendars of participants who switched on **free/busy sharing**
   (only busy times, never what the events are), and suggests times on weekdays between 09:00 and 18:00.
2. You pick which options to offer; Alfred asks each participant, on Telegram (tick the times that work, then
   **Send**, or **None work**) or by phone.
3. When everyone has answered, the earliest time that suits all is booked in your Google Calendar with invitations
   to the participants, and everyone is told. If no time works, you get the answers.

If someone is in their quiet hours, Alfred asks you first whether to send now anyway, send to the others now and
the rest later, or wait. Open requests are listed on **Tasks → Meetings** (you can withdraw them). Calls to
businesses are listed on **Tasks → Outside calls** (you can cancel them).

## When someone unknown calls

**Who:** for your information (admins and cofounders receive the messages).

If a number that isn't in the registry calls Alfred, he can answer as a polite assistant: without naming anyone,
he says the call is recorded, asks who's calling, who the message is for and what it's about, then says goodbye.
The message arrives on Telegram (for the person it was meant for, plus the admins) with a **Block this number**
button. Withheld and blocked numbers get a busy tone. Admins can switch this off and manage blocked numbers on
**Settings → Call screening**.
