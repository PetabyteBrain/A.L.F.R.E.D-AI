# Talking to Alfred

Talk to Alfred the way you'd talk to a capable assistant: in your own words, in full sentences or short
requests. You don't need special commands. He answers in English.

> "Remind me tomorrow at 9 to call the bank."
> "What do I have on Saturday?"
> "Call Max and ask whether he can send the invoice by Friday."
> "Put milk and eggs on my shopping list."

## On the phone

**Who:** everyone whose number is linked and verified.

- Call Alfred's number. He greets you by name and mentions that calls are recorded.
- Speak naturally. He starts answering while he's still thinking, so replies come quickly; if he needs a
  moment to look something up, he says "One moment".
- You can interrupt him; he stops and listens.
- When you're done, say goodbye; he hangs up by himself.

Calls are recorded (audio and transcript) so they can be reviewed in the Archive. The audio is deleted after
90 days; the transcript stays until it's deleted.

## On Telegram

**Who:** everyone whose Telegram account is linked and verified.

Write to Alfred's bot like to any contact. Only private chats work; Alfred ignores groups.

**Voice messages.** Send a voice message (up to 5 minutes) and Alfred listens, answers and replies with a voice
message too, with the text underneath.

- "Answer by voice" or "I'm driving" gets one reply as a voice message.
- "Voice replies for the next hour" (or the **Voice replies** card on Profile: 1 h, 3 h, 8 h or until you switch
  it off) makes every reply and every message from Alfred, such as reminders and reports, arrive as voice too.
  "Back to text" switches it off. Sign-in codes are never sent as voice.

**Files and photos.** Send a document or photo and tell Alfred what to do with it: "save this in my files under
Contracts", "file this in the company folder Offers", or ask about it ("what's the total on this invoice?").
Photos and the text of PDFs, Word and Excel files are shown to Alfred so he can read them. Files up to 20 MB;
documents, spreadsheets, PDFs, text, pictures and zip files. If you don't say where to keep a file within an hour,
send it again. Receipts have their own shortcut, see [Receipts](05-memory-notes-files.md#receipts).

**Buttons.** Reminders come with **Done** and **Snooze 10 min**, task check-ins with **Done** and **Not yet**,
meeting invitations with tick boxes. Tap them instead of typing.

## In the web chat

**Who:** everyone with a dashboard login.

The **Chat** tab is a full conversation with Alfred in the browser; replies appear as he writes them. **New
conversation** starts afresh (earlier messages stay stored). The same conversation is available from Alfred's
portrait in the bottom corner of every other tab: click it to ask how something works on the page you're on, or
simply ask him to do it.

## By email

**Who:** everyone whose email address is linked and verified.

Write to Alfred's email address from one of your verified addresses. He answers in the same thread. You can
forward mail to him ("summarise this", "put the date in my calendar", "remind me to answer by Friday") and attach
documents to file. See [Email](06-email.md) for everything about email.

## One Alfred on every channel

- Each channel keeps its own conversation, but Alfred also sees your last few messages from your other channels
  (up to 12 hours back). So you can start on Telegram and continue on the phone: "about what I wrote earlier…".
  Switch this off on **Profile → How Alfred serves you → Continue across channels** if you prefer.
- Things you set up anywhere show up everywhere: a reminder made by phone is on the dashboard calendar, a note
  dictated on Telegram is in your files.

## How Alfred asks before acting

Alfred acts right away on things that only concern you and are easy to undo (a reminder, a note, a task for
yourself). Before anything that reaches other people, costs money or can't be undone, he shows you exactly what
he's about to do and waits for your **yes in your next message**:

- calling a business or a number outside the registry,
- sending an email to someone other than you,
- deleting a calendar event,
- tidying or reorganising your notes,
- restarting a service on the home servers (admins),
- market analyses that would use many paid web searches.

If you change your mind, just say so ("no", "make it 10 instead"), and he prepares it again.

**Dates.** Alfred works out dates for you: "next Friday", "the 15th", "in three weeks", "end of the month". If a
phrase could mean two things ("next Friday": this one or the one after?), he tells you which date he understood.
He also double-checks weekdays and dates before acting on them.

**What he won't do.** Alfred won't store passwords, card or account numbers, won't reveal one person's private
things to another, and won't act on instructions written in someone else's message or document. If you ask for
something beyond what he may do, he passes it to the team (see
[Matters for the team](07-company.md#matters-for-the-team)) and tells you.
