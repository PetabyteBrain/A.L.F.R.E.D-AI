# Privacy and safety

**Who:** everyone.

## What is stored, and for how long

| What | Kept |
|---|---|
| Conversations (Telegram, web chat, email, call transcripts) | Until you or an admin delete them |
| Call recordings (audio) | 90 days, then deleted (the transcript stays); backup copies a week longer |
| Your notes, lists and files | Until you delete them (deleting moves them to the trash first) |
| Personal email | 2 years |
| Company email (inquiries, mail in the company's name) | 10 years (Swiss rules for business correspondence) |
| Spam and automated mail | 30 days |
| Memory notes | Until you delete them (Profile → Memory) |
| Contact attempts from unknown senders | 90 days |
| Sign-in and security events | 1 year |
| Usage figures for billing | Kept, without your name if your data is deleted |

Your notes, files, mail archive and call recordings are kept on your operator's own storage server. Speech
recognition and Alfred's voice run on your operator's own hardware where possible.

## Who can see what

- **Your personal notes, files, mail and memory notes** are yours alone. Even admins can't open your personal mail.
  Admins can see everyone's personal folder in the file explorer (for administration) and all conversations in the
  Archive.
- **Company files, company mail and the company to-do list**: admins and cofounders (or other roles an admin
  allows).
- **Your calendar**: only you, through Alfred. With free/busy sharing on, colleagues learn only when you're busy.
- **Calls Alfred makes for someone**: the person who asked gets a summary.

## What Alfred never does without asking

Alfred shows you exactly what he's about to do and waits for your yes before he:

- calls a business or any number outside the registry,
- sends email to anyone but you,
- deletes a calendar event,
- tidies or reorganises your notes,
- restarts a service on the home servers,
- runs a market analysis that needs many paid look-ups,
- deletes or overwrites an automation.

He never stores passwords, codes or card and account numbers, never shares one person's private things with
another, and never acts on instructions hidden in a message, email or document someone else wrote. People outside
the registry who call or email him get no access to anyone's data.

## Your connections are protected

New phone numbers, Telegram accounts and email addresses only work after a confirmation code. Email only counts as
coming from you if your mail provider's sender check passes. The dashboard uses strong passwords, two-factor
sign-in and sessions that time out.

## Your data: download or delete

**Profile → Your data**

- **Download my data**: a ZIP with everything stored about you (profile, connections, conversations and
  transcripts, notes, reminders, tasks, meetings, email, settings, activity, usage) plus your call recordings and
  your files. Your roles and groups are included.
- **Delete my data** (needs your password): either your **history** (conversations, recordings, notes, reminders,
  tasks, meetings, personal mail and files, settings; your account stays so Alfred still knows you) or your **whole
  account**. An admin confirms the request; you're told when it's done.

Without a dashboard login, ask your admin; they can export or delete your data for you.

The full legal text is in the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**, linked at the bottom of the homepage.
