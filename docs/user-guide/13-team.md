# Team: employees, time off and onboarding

**Who:** admins and cofounders by default. The permissions are separate (*Employees: …*): your **own** record and
time off, the **directory** and team calendar, **managing** employees, and **approving** changes. An admin can give,
for example, every employee just their own record, so they can ask for holidays themselves (see
[Roles and permissions](09-administration.md#roles-and-permissions)).

The **Team** tab keeps the people who work for the company: who they are, who they report to, who's away, how many
holidays they have left, and what's still to do when someone joins or leaves. **Every change to employee data waits
for approval**, time off included; ticking off checklist steps is simply work and applies at once.

## By chat, phone or email

> "Book me a holiday from 2 to 6 November." · "I'm sick today." · "How many holidays do I have left?"
>
> "Cancel my holiday next week." · "Who's off next week?" · "Who's away today?"
>
> "Add Erika Mustermann as a designer starting 1 November, with onboarding."
>
> "Erika's last day is 31 March, start the offboarding." · "What's left on Erika's onboarding?"
>
> "The laptop for Erika is set up, tick it off."

Alfred tells you when something waits for approval, and you hear the decision on Telegram.

## The Team tab

Views on the left: **Me**, **Directory**, **Absences**, **Checklists**, **Teams**, for people who manage personnel
**Accounts** and **Access log**, and **Approvals**, plus who's **away today**.

### Me

Your own record: **holidays** for the year (taken, planned, waiting for approval, and what's left), your time off,
and **Request time off**. Holidays are counted in working days: weekends and public holidays don't count, and a single
day can be a half day. If you start or leave during the year, your holidays are worked out for the part of the year
you're employed, plus any days carried over.

### Directory

Everyone with their title, department, manager and status: **starting soon**, **onboarding**, **active**, **leaving**
or **former** (tick *Former employees* to see them), and who's away. Open someone for:

- **Details**: work email and phone, employment and workload, first and last day, manager and team, their Alfred
  account, holidays a year.
- **Holidays** for the year and their **time off** (upcoming, earlier and declined). **Withdraw** a request that's
  still waiting; **Cancel…** an approved one that hasn't passed (that needs approval too).
- **Onboarding / offboarding** checklist, if one is running (see below).
- **Responsibilities**: their open tasks, the clients they own and their open deals.
- **Time tracked** this week and month, by client (when the time-tracking app is linked).
- **History**: every change, and who approved it.

**New employee** and **Edit** send the change for approval. Linking someone to their **Alfred account** is what lets
them see their own record, ask for time off, and get their checklist steps. A new employee can start with the
**onboarding** checklist; setting a last day can start **offboarding** (More ▾ on their page does the same).

### Absences

A month of the whole team: one row per person, their holidays, sick leave, training and other absences in colour,
striped while still waiting for approval, weekends and public holidays shaded. Below it, the month's absences as a
list.

Approved time off also shows in the morning briefing ("Erika is away today"), and **Alfred doesn't put calls others
ask for through to someone who is away**: he says when they're back and offers to call then, unless it's urgent. A
call that was already planned waits until they're back, and whoever asked for it is told.

### Checklists

Onboarding and offboarding run as checklists: each step has a **due date** (counted from the first or last day) and a
**person responsible**, who gets it in the morning briefing or a Telegram message on the day it's due. Tick steps off
on the employee's page or here (**All** or **My steps**); add a step, assign someone else, remove one, or **close** a
checklist early. A checklist closes by itself when everything is done.

### Approvals

New employees, changes to their details, time off and cancelling approved time off. Approvers also get each request
on Telegram with **Approve** / **Reject** buttons. You may approve your own.

### Teams

The teams that own work on the [boards](14-boards.md): a name, a colour (the team's lane), a **manager** who accepts
or declines the team's task requests, and its members. People who may manage teams create and change them here.

### Accounts and Access log

For people who manage personnel (admins by default): **Accounts** lists everyone Alfred knows with their roles,
groups, phone, Telegram and email, dashboard login and two-factor sign-in, and the data requests; **Add person**,
**Edit** and **Call** work as before. On an employee's page, their **Alfred account** link opens it here. **Access
log** shows who tried to reach Alfred, allowed or blocked. See [Administration](09-administration.md#people-and-roles).

### Holidays & checklists

For whoever manages employees: the holiday days a year for new employees, the **public holidays** (they never count
as days off), and the **onboarding** and **offboarding** templates: the steps, how many days before or after the day
each is due, and who is responsible (whoever starts it, their manager or the employee).

## Time tracking

If your company uses the time-tracking app, Alfred reads the hours from it (it never changes anything there): per
employee on the Team tab, and per client in the CRM and its reports. An employee's hours are found by their name in
the app (or the time-tracking name on their record); a client's by the session's tag (its name, or the time-tracking
tag set on the company).
