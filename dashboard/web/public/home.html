Alfred, a private AI butler

Alfred is a personal assistant you can phone, message on Telegram, or manage from a web dashboard. It answers questions, places and schedules phone calls on your behalf, keeps track of tasks and runs home and business automations. If you link your Google Calendar, it can also manage your calendar when you ask.

Alfred is a private, invitation-only service run by Erika Mustermann in Switzerland. It is not open for public sign-up: only people registered by the operator can use it.

What Alfred does

Answers the phone

Registered people can call Alfred's number and talk to it naturally. Calls are transcribed and recorded so they can be reviewed later.

Calls on your behalf

Ask Alfred to phone a registered contact now or at a set time ("call Max tomorrow at 9 and ask about the invoice"). It makes the call, then reports the answer back to you.

Chats on Telegram

Send Alfred a message for quick questions, reminders and tasks, and receive reports of calls and automations.

Manages your calendar

Optionally link your own Google Calendar so Alfred can tell you what's scheduled and add, move or remove events when you ask.

Runs automations

Alfred can trigger and report on automations (n8n workflows), such as daily weather alerts.

Dashboard

A secure web dashboard shows live calls and chats, recordings and transcripts, scheduled calls, tasks, system health and usage, and lets people manage their profile.

How Alfred uses Google Calendar

Linking a calendar is optional and done by each person from their own profile. Alfred requests access to calendar events (calendar.events) and your email address, and uses them only to:

  • show you your upcoming events when you ask;
  • create, change or delete events on your own calendar at your request (deleting requires your explicit confirmation).

Alfred never accesses anyone else's calendar through your link, never sells or shares calendar data for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. Tokens are stored encrypted, and you can unlink at any time, which also revokes Alfred's access. Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Privacy & security

Data is stored on a private server in Switzerland. Access is limited to registered people and their roles; the dashboard uses HTTPS, hashed passwords and secure sessions. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
