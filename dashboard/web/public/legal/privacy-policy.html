Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you use "Alfred", a private, invitation-only assistant service available by phone, messaging and a web dashboard. It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data we process

  • Identification and contact data of registered users, such as name, phone number, messaging account ID, email address and login credentials.
  • Communication content: messages you exchange with Alfred (voice messages are transcribed), and for phone calls an audio recording and a transcript.
  • Notes, lists and files you ask Alfred to keep, stored on our own storage server (NAS) in a folder only you can reach through Alfred; company files are only available to administrators and co-founders. This includes files and photos you send Alfred to keep (e.g. receipts); photos are also shown to the language model so it can read them.
  • Assistant data: reminders, tasks and their follow-ups, short notes Alfred keeps for you at your request, and your preferences such as quiet hours and the daily briefing (including the town you choose for the weather).
  • Calendar data, only if you choose to link your Google Calendar (see section 6).
  • Technical data, such as sign-in information, a session cookie, and aggregated usage information needed to operate the service.
  • Contact attempts from people who are not registered (phone number or account ID and time), to prevent misuse.
  • Calls from unknown numbers: if you call Alfred's number without being registered, Alfred may answer and take a message on behalf of its owner. The call is recorded and transcribed (Alfred says so at the start), and a short summary with your number is sent to the administrators. Withheld numbers are not answered. You can ask for these records to be deleted (section 10).
  • Calls Alfred makes to businesses or people outside its users at a user's request (for example to book a table): the number called, the task, a recording and transcript of the call, and its outcome. Alfred says at the start that it is an AI assistant calling on someone's behalf and that the call is recorded. If you ask not to be called again, your number is blocked.
  • Email: messages sent to Alfred's email address and those Alfred sends (addresses, names, subject, content, attachments, and the sender checks reported by our mail provider). Mail from registered users is handled like their other messages. If you are not a registered user, Alfred may answer your email using only public information about the company and passes anything else to the administrators and co-founders, who reply themselves; Alfred tells you when that happens. Emails are kept on our own storage server.
  • Business contacts (CRM): for clients, prospects, partners and suppliers of the company, the business and contact details our team enters or asks Alfred to enter (company name, people's names and job titles, business email, phone, address, website, notes), and a record of our dealings with them (notes on calls and meetings; emails and calls with them are linked by address). Only the team members allowed to see a record can see it. Deleting or merging records needs a second person's approval. You can ask what we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted (section 10).
  • Public business listings for market analyses that administrators request: business name, category, address, public website and business contact details from OpenStreetMap, the businesses' own websites and web search (our own search service, which passes the business name and town to public search engines, and the language model's web search), plus a short check of each website (for example whether it works on phones). The results are kept as a report in the company files and used only to identify potential clients; Alfred does not contact these businesses. Businesses can ask us to remove their entry at the contact address above.

3. Purposes

  • Providing the service: understanding and answering your requests, placing and handling calls, sending reminders, briefings and task follow-ups and, if linked, managing your calendar. Unless you switch it off on your profile, Alfred also takes your own last few messages from your other channels (up to 12 hours back) into account, so a conversation can continue from one channel to another; they are never shown to anyone else.
  • Letting you and, for calls or task follow-ups done on someone's behalf, the person who requested them review the outcome (transcripts, short summaries, and whether a task is done).
  • Confirming that a phone number or messaging account added to Alfred belongs to its user (a one-time code).
  • Passing on messages from unknown callers to the people they are meant for, and making calls our users ask for to get a specific task done.
  • Managing the company's business relationships (CRM): keeping track of clients, offers and what was agreed.
  • Operating, securing and improving the reliability of the service, and preventing misuse.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the performance of the service you use (Art. 6(1)(b)), our legitimate interest in a secure service and in managing our business relationships (Art. 6(1)(f)) and, for call recordings and calendar linking, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time.

4. Recipients

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. To provide the service we use carefully selected service providers, who process data on our behalf, in these categories:

  • providers of AI language processing;
  • providers of speech recognition and speech synthesis;
  • telephony, messaging and email providers (our email provider stores incoming mail for at most 72 hours until Alfred has collected it);
  • calendar provider (Google), only for users who link their calendar;
  • a weather service, which receives only the town you choose for your daily briefing;
  • hosting, network and security providers.

When Alfred calls someone on a user's behalf, a short summary of that call is shared with the user who requested it.

5. Disclosure abroad

Some of these providers process data in the United States and in member states of the European Union / EEA. For countries without an adequate level of data protection recognised by Switzerland, we rely on the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, or on standard contractual clauses recognised by the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

6. Google user data

Linking your Google Calendar is optional. If you link it, Alfred receives access to your calendar events and your Google account's email address, and uses them only to show, create, change or delete events on your own calendar when you ask it to. Calendar details are passed to our AI processing provider only as far as needed to answer your request. You can unlink at any time, which ends Alfred's access and deletes the stored access credentials.

Only if you turn on free/busy sharing on your profile (off by default), Alfred may also use the times at which your calendar is busy, never the event details, to suggest meeting times when another registered user asks to meet you. When you accept a time, the organiser's calendar sends you an invitation to your Google account's email address. You can turn sharing off at any time.

Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to develop or train generalised AI or machine-learning models, and not read by humans except with your consent, for security purposes, or where required by law.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, as long as you are a registered user, or as required by law. Audio recordings of calls are deleted 90 days after the call; their transcripts are kept until you delete them. Calendar access is kept until you unlink it, and notes Alfred keeps for you until you delete them (you can do this yourself on your profile). Records of unregistered contact attempts are deleted after 90 days. Emails are kept for 2 years, and business correspondence of the company for 10 years as Swiss law requires; spam and automated messages for 30 days. Business contact records (CRM) are kept while the business relationship lasts and afterwards as long as Swiss law requires for business records (up to 10 years), unless you ask us to delete them earlier where the law allows. Backup copies are kept on our own hardware: database copies for 14 days, recordings for at most a week longer than the recordings themselves.

8. Security

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures against unauthorised access, loss and misuse, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords and two-factor sign-in for the dashboard (a code from an authenticator app or sent by Telegram; the authenticator key is stored encrypted).

9. Cookies

The website only uses a strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in, plus display preferences stored in your own browser. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

You can request information about your data, its correction or deletion, a copy of data you provided, and object to or restrict processing; you can withdraw consent at any time. If you have a dashboard login, you can download a copy of your data and ask for its deletion yourself on your profile ("Your data"); deletions are confirmed by an administrator. Otherwise, contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You may also complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, to your local supervisory authority.
