ALFRED

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026 · Applies to the "Alfred" personal assistant service at alfred.example.com, its phone number, its Telegram bot and its dashboard.

In short: Alfred is a private, invitation-only assistant. It only serves people who have been registered by its operator. It processes what you say or write to it, call recordings and transcripts, and (only if you link it) your Google Calendar, in order to answer you and carry out what you ask. Your data is stored on a private server in Switzerland, is never sold, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

The controller responsible for processing personal data under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Erika Mustermann, Switzerland
Email: contact@example.com

2. What Alfred is

Alfred is an AI assistant that can be reached by phone call, via Telegram and through a web dashboard. Access is limited to people the operator has registered. Messages and calls from anyone else are ignored; such attempts are logged (see section 3.5).

3. Data we process

3.1 Registration data

Your name, role, and the identifiers used to recognise you: phone number, Telegram user ID, email address and, if you have dashboard access, a username with a securely hashed password (Argon2id). We never store your password in readable form.

3.2 Conversations

  • Telegram: the text of your messages and Alfred's replies.
  • Phone calls: an audio recording of the call (caller and Alfred), a written transcript, the call time and duration. Calls are recorded so that you (and, for calls placed on someone's behalf, the person who requested the call) can review what was said.
  • Actions: the tools Alfred used during a conversation (for example "scheduled a call", "created a calendar event") and their results.
  • Call requests: if someone asks Alfred to call you, the purpose of that call, the time, and a short summary of the outcome, which is shared with the person who asked.

3.3 Google Calendar data (only if you link your calendar)

If you choose to link your Google account on the dashboard, Alfred requests the scope calendar.events and your email address. With it, Alfred can read, create, change and delete events on your own calendar only when you ask it to (for example "what's on my calendar tomorrow?" or "add a meeting on Friday at 10"). Alfred never accesses another person's calendar through your link, and other users cannot use your calendar.

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted. You can unlink at any time on the dashboard (Profile), which deletes the tokens and revokes Alfred's access at Google; you can also revoke access in your Google account settings.

Google API Services — Limited Use. Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the calendar features you request; it is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train or improve generalised AI or machine-learning models, and humans do not read it except with your consent, for security purposes, or where required by law. Calendar details are passed to our AI provider (section 5) only as needed to answer your request, under terms that do not allow them to be used for model training.

3.4 Usage and technical data

Counts of API usage (for example tokens processed, speech characters, call minutes) to monitor costs; a session cookie when you sign in to the dashboard; IP address and browser type of dashboard logins, kept for security.

3.5 Unregistered access attempts

If an unregistered phone number or Telegram account contacts Alfred, we log the number or ID, the channel and the time, to detect misuse. These entries are deleted after 90 days.

4. Why we process it (purposes and legal bases)

  • To provide the assistant you or your organisation asked for: answering, calling, scheduling, calendar management (performance of the service / legitimate interest; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse (legitimate interest; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Recording calls and linking Google Calendar: based on your consent, which you give by using the phone service after being informed here and by actively linking your calendar (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time.

6. Where your data is stored and how long

Registration data, transcripts, recordings, call requests and logs are stored on a private server operated by the controller in Switzerland.

  • Registration data: while you are registered; deleted when your registration is removed.
  • Conversations, recordings and transcripts: until deleted by the operator or at your request. A fixed retention period will be introduced when archived storage is set up.
  • Dashboard sessions: expire after 60 minutes of inactivity and at most 12 hours.
  • Unregistered access attempts: 90 days.
  • Google tokens: until you unlink your calendar or access is revoked.

7. Security

Access is limited to registered people and roles. The website uses HTTPS only, hashed passwords, server-side sessions with secure cookies, CSRF protection and a strict content security policy. Third-party tokens are encrypted at rest. Phone webhooks are verified with Twilio signatures.

8. Cookies

The dashboard sets one strictly necessary session cookie when you sign in. It is used only to keep you logged in. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may also store your dashboard preferences (selected theme and tab) locally; these never leave your device.

9. Your rights

You can request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and object to processing, and withdraw any consent. Email contact@example.com. You can also correct most of your details yourself on the dashboard (Profile). You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, with your local supervisory authority.

10. Children

Alfred is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly register them.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be communicated to registered users.
