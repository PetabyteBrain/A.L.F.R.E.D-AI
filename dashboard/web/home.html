Capabilities

What Alfred does

01

Answers the phone

Call Alfred and talk naturally; it starts answering while it's still thinking. Every call is recorded and transcribed, and Alfred says so at the start. Unknown callers get a polite assistant who takes a message instead of a busy tone.

02

Calls on your behalf

“Call Max tomorrow at nine and ask about the invoice.” Alfred makes the call and reports the answer back to you, and it never rings anyone during their quiet hours unless you say it's urgent. It can also ring a business for one concrete task, like booking a table, after you've said yes.

03

Chats by text or voice

Message Alfred or use the web chat, where replies stream in as they're written. Send a voice message and Alfred answers with one, or ask for answers as voice notes when you can't read. Start on one channel and carry on in another: a call can pick up where a chat left off.

04

Its own email address

Email Alfred like a colleague: forward a message to summarise, a date to put in your calendar, a question to answer. It replies in the same thread, sends mail for you after you've seen it, and files every message. Mail from people it doesn't know gets a courteous answer from public information, and anything that needs a decision goes straight to the team.

05

Reminders that stick

“Remind me every weekday at 8:30.” A message with Done and Snooze buttons, or a phone call. Ignore it and Alfred can ring you instead.

06

Morning briefing

At a time you choose: the weather, your calendar, today's reminders and calls, your team's top to-dos and anything that happened overnight. By message or read to you on a call.

07

Follows up on tasks

“Ask Max to send the invoice by Friday and follow up.” When it's due, Alfred checks in with Max, asks again if needed, and tells you the outcome.

08

Keeps your diary

Your appointments, reminders, tasks and planned calls in one calendar, by month, week or day; click a day to add something. Link your Google Calendar and Alfred adds, moves or removes events when you ask, and “what do I have on Saturday?” always gets the full picture.

09

Remembers what matters

Tell Alfred once how you like things. It keeps short notes, uses them only with you, and you can see and edit them at any time.

10

Notes, lists & files

“Add milk to my shopping list.” “Note for the team: launch on the 15th.” Your notes and lists live on our own storage, in a space only you can reach; send a document or a receipt photo and Alfred files it where you say.

11

Answers from your documents

“What does our offer say about delivery time?” Alfred searches your files by meaning, in English or German, reads the right one and tells you which file the answer came from.

12

Finds new clients

“Find hairdressers within 10 km with no or a weak website.” Alfred goes through public business listings, checks each website, scores who could use the team's help and files a report with an Excel list. Contacting them stays your decision.

13

Knows your clients

“What's open with Acme?” “Acme signed, mark it won.” Clients and the people at them, a sales pipeline with a forecast, and a timeline of every note, call, meeting and email. New leads are suggested from market analyses and inquiries, every change can be undone, and deleting always waits for a colleague's approval.

14

Command dashboard

Live calls and chats, a searchable archive, Alfred's mailbox with groups and tags, notes and files, the calendar, tasks, scheduled calls, automations and system health in one place, with roles and groups you shape yourself, protected by two-factor sign-in. Four looks to choose from: Stark, Batcave, Matrix or Alfred.

15

At hand on the dashboard

Alfred himself waits in the corner of every page. Ask him how something works, or simply have him do it; his portrait shows what he's up to, from checking his tablet to a quiet cup of tea.

Protocol

How it works

  1. 1

    You ask

    Call, send a text, a voice message, a photo or an email, or use the dashboard: “Remind me at five”, “what's my day like?”, “call Max Mustermann and ask if he's coming”, “save this receipt”.

  2. 2

    Alfred acts

    It understands the request, checks what it's allowed to do for you, and handles it: a call, an email, a reminder, a task with a follow-up, a note, a file, a calendar change, an automation. Anything beyond its authority goes to a person, and you're told so.

  3. 3

    You get the result

    Answers come back where you asked. Calls made and tasks followed up for you are reported back, at a considerate hour.

Google Calendar

Your calendar, only when you ask

Linking a Google Calendar is optional and done by each person from their own profile. Alfred uses calendar access and your account's email address only to show, create, change or delete events on your own calendar at your request, and deleting always needs your confirmation. If you choose to share your free/busy for meeting scheduling, others only ever learn when you're busy, never what the events are. You can unlink or stop sharing at any time.

Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models.

Privacy

Private by design

Access is limited to registered people and what their roles allow (each permission switched on or off per role by the team), and a new phone number, messaging account or email address only works once it's confirmed with a code; an email only counts as yours if it passes the sender checks. No advertising, no tracking cookies, no selling of data. Speech recognition, Alfred's voice, your notes, files and recordings stay on our own servers, and the dashboard needs two-factor sign-in. Calls are recorded and transcribed so they can be reviewed; the audio is deleted after 90 days. Everyone can see and correct their own details, set quiet hours when Alfred won't disturb them, choose whether Alfred may carry a conversation over from one channel to another, and download or delete their data from their profile.
